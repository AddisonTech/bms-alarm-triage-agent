# Which readings show the fault?

Thanks for doing this. It should take about 20 minutes.

## What I am asking

For each fault listed below, tick every reading you would expect to look
wrong while that fault is happening. Wrong meaning out of range, drifting,
stuck, or otherwise not what you would expect on a healthy unit.

That is the whole task. There are 28 faults, grouped by equipment type,
and the same five readings apply to every fault within a group.

## How to think about it

Tick a reading if a technician looking at a day of trend data would see
that reading misbehave because of this fault.

- Tick as many as apply. Most faults show up in more than one.
- Leave it blank if the reading would look normal, or if it would only
  move a little for reasons unrelated to the fault.
- If you genuinely cannot say, write a `?` instead of a tick. A question
  mark is more useful to me than a guess.
- If a fault would show up in something that is not on the list, write it
  in the Notes line. I would rather know the list is incomplete.

Do not worry about how severe the fault is, how long it takes to show up,
or what anyone should do about it. Only which readings look wrong.

There are no right answers I am checking you against. Your answers become
the reference.
